# Behavioral Interview Questions

**Mentorship session:** November 9, 2026

Use this folder for behavioral interview practice and STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) answers.

## Suggested question bank

1. Tell me about yourself and the impact of your recent work.
2. Describe a time you disagreed with a stakeholder about a technical decision.
3. Tell me about a production issue you diagnosed and resolved.
4. Describe how you handled ambiguity or incomplete requirements.
5. Give an example of influencing a team without formal authority.
6. Describe a trade-off between delivery speed, data quality and maintainability.
7. Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned.
8. Describe a time you improved reliability, cost or performance and quantified the outcome.

## Answer template

- **Situation:** Business and technical context (1–2 sentences).
- **Task:** Your responsibility and success criteria.
- **Action:** Specific decisions **you** made, including alternatives and stakeholder communication.
- **Result:** Measurable outcome and lesson learned.
